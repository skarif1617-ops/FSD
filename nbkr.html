About NBKRIST

NBKR Institute of Science & Technology is an autonomous engineering institution located at Vidyanagar, Andhra Pradesh.

The institute provides undergraduate and postgraduate education in engineering and technology disciplines.

NBKRIST focuses on technical education, innovation, academic development, industry interaction and student development.

The campus provides various academic, residential, sports and student-support facilities.
